# NimStreak

> Stake NIM on your habits. Complete your streak, earn rewards. Miss a day, lose your stake.

🎯 Live: https://nimstreak.vercel.app

## What Is NimStreak

NimStreak is a habit accountability app built on Nimiq. Users stake real NIM on personal goals — fitness, learning, coding, health. Complete your daily check-in every day and finish the challenge to earn your stake back plus a bonus from everyone who quit. Miss one day and lose your stake to the winners pool.

## How It Works

1. Connect your Nimiq wallet inside NimiqPay
2. Create or join a habit challenge
3. Stake NIM — minimum 0.5 NIM
4. Check in every day to prove you did the habit
5. Miss a day — your stake goes to the winners pool
6. Complete the full challenge — get your stake back plus bonus NIM

## Game Modes

- **Solo Challenge** — just you vs yourself
- **Group Challenge** — invite friends, compete together
- **Public Challenge** — join open community challenges

## Tech Stack

- Frontend: React + Vite + GSAP animations
- Backend: Node.js + Express + Socket.IO
- Database: PostgreSQL + Redis
- Wallet: Nimiq Pay + Nimiq Hub API
- Payments: Direct NIM transfers via Nimiq RPC
- Deployment: Vercel + Railway
